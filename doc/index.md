## What is Gnomon?

**Gnomon** is an open-source platform that allows to intuitively design reproducible computational pipelines for the analysis and simulation of morphogenesis. It manipulates 3D+t representations of developing living forms, and lets the user seamlessly interoperate plugins to visualize and process them through dedicated workspaces. 

The Gnomon development project is ongoing, and is carried out by the [Mosaic Inria project-team](https://team.inria.fr/mosaic) hosted by the [RDP Laboratory](https://www.ens-lyon.fr/RDP/?lang=en) at the ENS de Lyon.
